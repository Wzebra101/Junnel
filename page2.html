Nutrition Month

Kain muna bago laro! Fuel the player before the tip-off.

What Is Nutrition Month?

Nutrition Month is celebrated every July in the Philippines to promote good nutrition and healthy eating habits. The celebration is led by the National Nutrition Council (NNC), which encourages schools, families, and communities to make healthier food choices and stay active.

For a basketball player, the message is simple: the court rewards the ones who fuel their bodies well. Every sprint, jump, and pivot starts with what is on your plate.

Why Nutrition Matters on the Court

Basketball is a game of constant motion. Players run up and down the floor, jump for rebounds, and stop and change direction in a split second. All of that movement needs energy, strong muscles, and a sharp mind.

Eating well helps a player keep stamina through the fourth quarter, recover after hard practices, and stay focused when the game is on the line. Teenagers are still growing, so good food also supports strong bones and healthy development.

Reminder: a player who skips meals runs out of gas early. Skipping breakfast on game day is like starting the game with a flat ball.

The Six Essential Nutrients

  • Carbohydrates: The main source of energy for sprints and fast breaks.
  • Proteins: Build and repair muscles after practice and games.
  • Fats: Provide long-lasting energy and protect the body's organs.
  • Vitamins: Help the body stay healthy and fight off sickness.
  • Minerals: Strengthen bones and teeth, and keep the body working properly.
  • Water: Keeps the body hydrated and carries nutrients to where they are needed.

Game-Day Eating Plan

Timing matters as much as the menu. Here is a simple plan a player can follow from the morning to the final buzzer.

Moment What to Eat and Drink Why It Helps
Before the game Rice, whole wheat bread, eggs, and a banana Gives steady energy for the whole game.
Halftime Water and a piece of fruit Refills fluids and gives a quick energy boost.
After the game Fish or chicken with rice and vegetables, plus milk Helps the muscles recover and rebuild.
Every day Plenty of fruits and vegetables Supplies the vitamins and minerals the body needs.

Healthy Habits of a Champion

  • Eat balanced meals every day.
  • Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables.
  • Drink water before, during, and after practice.
  • Stay physically active and train regularly.
  • Get enough sleep every night so your body can recover.
  • Limit junk food, soft drinks, and sweets.
  • Wash your hands before eating.

Conclusion

Nutrition Month reminds every player that good health begins with good food. Practice builds skill, but nutrition gives the body what it needs to use that skill. By eating well, drinking enough water, and staying active, anyone can play stronger and live healthier.

Champions are built in the kitchen as much as on the court.

Eat Right, Play Strong, Stay Healthy!
