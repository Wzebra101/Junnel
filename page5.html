CSS Border

Draw the court lines, one border at a time.

What Is a Border?

A CSS border is the line drawn around an element. On a basketball court, borders are everywhere: the sideline, the baseline, the free-throw line, and the three-point arc. Each line tells the players where they can go and what counts.

With CSS you control three things about every border: its width, its style, and its color.

Border Styles

The border-style property changes how the line looks. Here are the eight most common styles, each named after a part of the game.

The Sidelineborder-style: solid
Free-Throw Lineborder-style: dashed
Dribble Trailborder-style: dotted
Double Teamborder-style: double
Hardwood Grooveborder-style: groove
Rim Ridgeborder-style: ridge
Pressed Floorborder-style: inset
Raised Backboardborder-style: outset

Border Width and Color

The border-width property sets how thick the line is. You can use keywords like thin, medium, and thick, or an exact size like 16px. The border-color property sets the color.

Jump Ballborder-width: thin
Tip-Offborder-width: medium
Full Court Pressborder-width: thick
Buzzer Beaterborder-width: 16px

Border Sides

You can style each side separately with border-top, border-right, border-bottom, and border-left. You can also give the four sides different widths and colors in one rule.

Top of the Keyborder-top
Baselineborder-bottom
Left Wingborder-left
Right Wingborder-right
Four Different Sidesborder-width: 4px 12px 20px 28px

Rounded Borders

The border-radius property rounds the corners. A small radius softens the box, and a radius of 50% on a square turns it into a perfect circle, which is exactly how you draw a basketball.

Square Cornersborder-radius: 0
Soft Cornersborder-radius: 18px
Pill Shapeborder-radius: 999px
border-radius: 50%

The Border Shorthand

Instead of writing three separate properties, you can combine them in one line. The order is width, then style, then color.

border: 4px solid #f26a1b;

border-width: 4px;
border-style: solid;
border-color: #f26a1b;
Property What It Controls On the Court
border-width How thick the line is How bold the painted line looks
border-style The pattern of the line Solid sideline or dashed free-throw line
border-color The color of the line The team colors on the floor
border-radius How round the corners are From square court to round ball

Conclusion

Borders do more than decorate. They organize a page the same way painted lines organize a court. With the right width, style, color, and radius, you can mark every area clearly and make your design look game-ready.

Stay inside the lines, then style them.
